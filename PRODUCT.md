# Home Again Cleaning Services — product

## Platform
- Platform: web
- Stack: static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), hosted on GitHub Pages
- Primary device: phones, usually inside Facebook's in-app browser

## Users
- Homeowners and renters in Ocean County, NJ, and surrounding areas who want regular, deep or one-time cleaning.
- Airbnb and vacation-rental hosts who need turnovers between guests (inferred from the listed service; Ocean County shore towns).
- Small offices and commercial spaces.
- The business owner first: the site is given to them free and has to show them their own business, done well, on one phone screen.

## Purpose
Turn a single text-only Facebook group post into a real home for the business: say what they clean and where, and make the free, no-obligation estimate easy to ask for.

## Positioning
A locally owned Ocean County cleaner that tailors each clean to the home and is dependable and detail-oriented (their own words). Warm and personal, not corporate.

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact today is Facebook Messenger only: no phone, no email, no business page (source: Muse brief 2026-10-20). The estimate form composes a message, copies it, and opens Messenger. When a phone number is confirmed, `CONFIG.PHONE` switches the header, form and floating circle to call/text.
- No photos, no logo, no reviews, no prices exist. The page claims none of them.
- The poster's (Lynne) relationship to the business is unconfirmed: the page names no owner.
- No tracking, no frameworks. One external resource: Cal Sans from Google Fonts.

## Brand commitments
- Name: "Home Again Cleaning Services" (post sign-off: "Home Again LLC").
- Voice: "we / us", warm and personal, matching their captions.
- Their own lines, usable verbatim: "Because a clean home is more than just a luxury — it's peace of mind."; "Let us handle the mess, so you can enjoy the moments that matter most."; "Bringing the comfort back home, one clean space at a time."
- Colours: none published. Navy and a warm sparkle gold are chosen by the builder (inferred from the 🏡✨ in their post title).

## Evidence on hand
- Services (verbatim): Residential Home Cleaning; Deep Cleaning & Detailed Cleaning; Weekly, Biweekly & Monthly Cleaning; Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning; Airbnb & Vacation Rental Turnovers; Office & Commercial Cleaning; One-Time & Special Occasion Cleaning.
- Claims (verbatim): "Locally owned and operated"; "Personalized cleaning tailored to your needs"; "Dependable, detail-oriented service"; "NOW ACCEPTING NEW CLIENTS!"; "FREE, NO-OBLIGATION ESTIMATE!"
- Area: Ocean County, NJ, and surrounding areas.

## Principles
- Phone first; the first screen shows who, what, where and the estimate button.
- Only facts from the post. No invented reviews, years, prices, insurance or photos.
- One clear way to get in touch at a time.
